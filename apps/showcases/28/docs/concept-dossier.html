1. Visual Vision & Core Aesthetic Principle

"Darkness is the only way light reads as light."

The showcase represents a cinematic, precision photonics cleanroom. The physical centerpiece is the genuine Cauchy optical dispersion through an equilateral dense flint-glass prism (n ≈ 1.629–1.688). Real physics is truth; the dramatic presentation must serve this truth instead of drowning it in flat washed-out exposure.

2. Aesthetic Diagnosis (The "Fehlerbild")

Exposure & Bloom Blown Out Milky Self-Luminous Prism White Slab Phosphor Screen Stippled Metal Floor Noise TIR Wobble Truncation
  • Milky Self-Luminous Prism: The prism uses a semi-opaque StandardMaterial with emissiveColor and self-glow, completely hiding facet edges and reading like a plastic lightbulb rather than clear refractive glass.
  • Blown-Out Atmosphere: Excessive environment intensity (1.5) and low bloom threshold (0.75) with high intensity (1.4) bleach the whole frame. The incident beam clips to a harsh white bar.
  • Phosphor Screen as Detached Flare: The white receiver panel acts like a competing light emitter rather than a dark absorption detector where the spectrum lands.
  • TIR Truncation Bug: The turntable oscillation (±0.25 rad) at 44° incidence repeatedly pushes rays past the critical angle, causing spectral bands (especially violet/blue) to abruptly disappear during normal playback.
  • Bench Surface & Brass Ring: Noisy reflections on scratched steel, brass clip slicing into the turntable without contact shadow or mount recess.

4. Reference Architecture & Backends

Ensures full multi-backend parity (WebGL1, WebGL2, WebGPU). GlassMaterial is selected over StandardMaterial.transmission (which suffers from a WebGPU-only limitation in the engine). Spot light budgets are capped at ≤ 3 scene spots to stay within forward-shader per-material loop ceilings.
