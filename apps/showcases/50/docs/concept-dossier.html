The Interactive Chess Set

Remote glTF streaming (12 MB) Load-progress indicator (first public) Draco + KTX2 via opt-in package 32 draggable pieces + square snap showcase 50

Elevator Pitch & Design Pillars

The engine's first runtime asset-streaming experience: the official Khronos ABeautifulGame chess set (KTX-ETC1S-Draco GLB, ~12 MB) is pulled straight from GitHub in the browser with a visible, honest byte-stream progress bar, decoded live through the opt-in Draco + Basis Universal plugins, and handed over as a grabbable 3D chess board — every one of the 32 pieces can be picked up, dragged across the board and placed; a snap toggle locks them onto the 8×8 square lattice that is derived from the model's own piece positions, never from hardcoded constants.

  • Progress is honest: the indicator shows real downloaded bytes and the decode/prepare phases — the first public demonstration of how asset-load progress can be visualized in SWM (AssetManager.streamBinary).
  • Grab, don't click: pieces react with hover/grab scaling and lift; orbit camera yields while a piece is held (canRotate guard) so dragging never fights the view.
  • Derived, not hardcoded: the square lattice and field labels (a1..h8) come from the decoded scene data.
  • Opt-in package in its natural habitat: this is the first in-app consumer of @small-world-machine/gltf-extensions — compressed Khronos content decoded by the engine's own plugin registry.
  • Zero local footprint: the 43 MB uncompressed sibling stays uncommitted; nothing but the 56 KB IBL is part of the build.

Art Direction & Mood

A quiet, focused studio tableau: neutral graphite backdrop and neutral IBL put the marble and glass of the official set front and center. The dark theme of the load overlay and the Showcase Drawer panel follows the established lab convention (monospace telemetry), while the chess interaction itself stays completely free of UI chrome — grab, move, release.

Interactive Controls & UX

  • Left mouse — grab / drag a piece (hover feedback, lifted while moved)
  • R — reset camera · Shift R — reset board (all pieces home)
  • I / H — Interactive Chess Lab panel (Reset Board, Grid Snap toggle, live readout)
  • Option/Alt + 1/2/3 — renderer swap (WebGL1/2/WebGPU)

Technical Architecture

The load path is the engine's own streaming pipeline, composed — not a bespoke loader:

  • AssetManager.streamBinary(url, onChunk, onProgress) — read-stream fetch with byte-level progress (throttled to ~4 Hz for the overlay DOM).
  • GltfBinaryParser.parseGlb(bytes) → GltfLoader.parse(data, url) — Draco mesh decode + Basis Universal transcode happen inside the registered opt-in plugins (main-thread WASM by design of the package).
  • ChessSet (Pieces.ts) — inventory scan by node-name regex, lattice derivation from home positions, leaf-mesh picking forwarded to piece roots, snap/clamp constraint, reset/dispose.

Invariants: GLTF-ROOT-IDENTITY (verified), LATTICE-DERIVED, PIECE-MESH-LEAF-PICKING, SINGLE-CONSUMER-PER-LEAF.
