Showcase 41: Metal Wall Relief & Assemblage Art

Interactive 2.5D sculptural metal wall relief with palette-knife impasto facets, flex grind marks, physical tensioned wire strings, and vibrant color-pop accents.

1. Visual & Artistic Doctrine

Mixed-Media Assemblage

Replicates handcrafted metal wall reliefs: welded and bent tin/iron sheets, expanded diamond wire mesh (Streckmetall), tensioned guitar/steel wires, and thick impasto paint strokes.

Color-Pop / Selective Hue

90% of the composition is desaturated into cold gunmetal, zinc silver, and deep iron oxide/rust in shadows. A single hue (vivid scarlet red) commands full visual attention.

2.5D Diorama Parallax

Physical depth layering allows specular glints to travel across metal ridges and wire cables as the camera orbits, producing an authentic sculptural sensation unattainable with pure 2D filters.

2. Technical Architecture

  • Package: @small-world-machine/artistic-extras (isolated from engine core).
  • Material: MetalReliefMaterial supporting GLSL 300 es, GLSL 100, and WGSL.
  • Procedural Voronoi Normal Distortion: Calculates analytical 2D cell noise with randomized slopes per cell to mimic heavy palette-knife paint and hammered tin.
  • Edge Curvature Bevel: Uses screen-space derivatives (fwidth, dpdx/dpdy) to dynamically highlight welded borders as polished bare metal.
  • Post-Processor: MetalReliefPostProcessor providing standalone screen-space Sobel relief embossing and color-pop filtering.

3. Preset Motifs

  • Paris Rain: The iconic couple embracing under a red umbrella next to the Eiffel Tower and wet pavement.
  • Alpine Cable Car: Red mountain gondola suspended from high-tension overhead wire cables against faceted slate peaks.
  • Nordic Harbor: Colorful Nyhavn townhouses with a moored sailboat and real mast rigging strings.
