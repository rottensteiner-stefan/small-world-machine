Elevator Pitch & Design Pillars

An interactive laboratory that makes Small World Machine's next-generation particle system explorable — extended into a fire-45-style variant lab: the viewer (or a golden capture) selects one of six curated effect variants (overview/forge/smoke/trails/ weather/gpu) and one of three camera pools (overview/closeup/budget). The technical core stays: physics-grade SDF collision, divergence-free aerodynamic swirl, soft-particle depth integration, procedural volume smoke lighting, pooled particle lights, ribbon trails, WebGPU compute autonomy and roof-aware weather collision — composed from SWM's Godot/PlayCanvas-derived Affector architecture.

  • Demonstrate, don't decorate: every lane is a real, inspectable capability with live toggle — no pre-baked movie.
  • Zero-GC honesty: the lab runs the same hot paths tests assert (allocation-free frames).
  • Variant = composition, not rebuild: switching toggles lane visibility/emission + camera; systems are never disposed/recreated (no resource flips).
  • Tier honesty: the gpu variant is a real WebGPU compute lane behind WebGPURenderer+navigator.gpu; without a device (or when disabled) it honestly degrades to forge.
  • Golden-safe: deterministic captures via ?__golden=part46-<variant>-<pool>; does not perturb fire45/pbr38 baselines.

Art Direction & Mood

Industrial forge-atelier ambience: warm ember light on dark, grounded geometry; the webgl atlas-grey palette keeps silhouette and scatter readable. UI follows the Showcase Drawer convention (dark panels, monospace telemetry).

Technical Architecture

Lanes are independent ParticleSystem SoA simulations composed from Affector building blocks (SdfColliderAffector, CurlNoiseAffector, GravityAffector), rendered through ParticleMeshRenderer (active-prefix compaction, octagon cutout). Impact events flow through the SubEmitter ring buffer (zero-alloc). The lab layer: Variants.ts is the single spec source (variants, camera pools, robust resolver), Hud.ts owns the ShowcaseDrawer panel (shortcuts, sliders, readout), GoldenCapture.ts runs deterministic 1280×720 capture pools (part46-<variant>-<pool>). Emission is seed-pinned (mulberry32) and rate-based so every variant is observable and goldens are byte-stable. Invariants: SDF-NON-PENETRATION, CURL-DIVERGENCE-FREE, LIFETIME-CURVE-KEY-EXACTNESS, S34-POOL-PARTITION.
