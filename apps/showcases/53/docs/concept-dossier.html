Design Pillars

  • Brutalist Wedge Silhouette: Beveled multi-tier monolith rising out of a dark basalt sea foundation (`y = -6..52.8 m`), flanked by recessed crimson hangar bays, cantilevered landing pads, turbolasers, and imperial shuttles.
  • Calibrated CC0 PBR + Waterline Wetness: 2K WebP PBR maps (`MetalPlates017A`, `Metal046B`, `dark_rock_02`) driven by a showcase-local `CustomShaderMaterial` wrapping `Standard.frag.*` with an animated wave-lap wetness band.
  • Stormy Ocean Reflection: `OpenWaterMaterial` paired with `PlanarReflectionNode` and a deep seabed (`y = -14 m`) so Beer-Lambert absorption, shoreline foam, and red hangar reflections render cleanly across `WEB_GPU`, `WEB_GL2`, and `WEB_GL1`.
