Showcase 53 — Fortress Inquisitorius (Ocean Moon Nur)

1:1 canonical architectural reconstruction of the Obi-Wan Kenobi closed basalt monolith: a natural ~485 m Mustafar-like volcanic stone spire rising organically out of Nur's stormy ocean, carved at mid-elevation by an engineered Imperial durasteel through-flight hangar bay.

Decision 1A · Closed OWK Obelisk (No Top Split) Decision 2A · 1:1 Canon Scale (~485 m) Decision 3A · Human & Prop Scale Anchors Volcanic Stone Exterior vs. Durasteel Hangar HDR Crimson Core & Cool Gantry Trusses

1. Core Creative & Architectural Decisions

Natural Monolith vs. Human Bay

The outer sloped flanks (lower bastion and unbroken upper spire) are built as dark, horizontally stratified volcanic basalt stone — as though the obelisk grew naturally from the ocean floor. Human Imperial engineering appears strictly inside the cantilevered waist hangar, blast doors, pilaster frames, ceiling trusses, and waterline defensive plinths.

Closed Obi-Wan Kenobi Silhouette (1A)

Unlike the twin-blade apex cleft used in earlier prototypes, the upper obelisk rises continuously from the hangar overhang (`y = 140 m`) to a closed, flat-topped truncated volcanic summit (`y = 485 m`) at a `~2.0:1` height-to-base ratio (`485 m` vs. `248 m` waterline width), featuring only two subtle recessed comms/sensor housings on the top plateau.

Detailed Interior Landing Bay + Waterline

The cantilevered mid-waist landing bay (`y = 116..140 m`) is fully modeled: wet tiled durasteel deck plates (`~3.5 m` module), low perimeter safety barriers, projecting wing aprons, curved steel pilasters framing carved basalt cliff walls, 3 pairs of trapezoidal blast doors with vertical crimson slits, a central glowing red core portal, backlit gantry trusses, and 16 suspended TIE/ln fighters. Waterline basalt crags, sentry landing pad, and 4 scaled turbolasers are preserved.
