Atmospheric Sanctum: Volumetric Froxel Fog & Interior Carving

Interactive architectural benchmark demonstrating true 3D Froxel integration, CSM light shafts, negative density carving, and 3D procedural rolling mist.

Core Pillars & Architectural Highlights

1. True 3D Froxel Pipeline

Log-staggered depth slicing ($160 \times 90 \times 64$) coupled with clustered lighting. In-register optical depth integration evaluates Beer-Lambert extinction along Z, sampled in a single trilinear hardware lookup.

2. Negative Density Carving

Godot/Frostbite-style carving ($extinction = \max(0.0, extinction + volDensity)$). A Box FogVolume inside the rear vaulted chamber keeps the interior 100% fog-free while mist swirls outside.

3. 3D Procedural Rolling Mist

2-octave analytical gradient value noise with time-based wind drift. Creates billowy, swirling turbulence over the altar and incense braziers without consuming any texture memory.

Interactive Showcase Features

  • [F] Toggle Fog: Toggles global volumetric fog and directional light shafts.
  • [C] Toggle Carving: Toggles the negative density carving volume to compare a flooded room vs. a crystal-clear interior.
  • [N] Toggle 3D Noise: Switches between uniform laminar mist and turbulent rolling mist.
  • [1] / [2] Solar Alignment: Switches between vertical high-noon clerestory shafts and deep golden-hour diagonal rays.
  • [V] Camera Navigation: Switches between OrbitController and FlyController to explore the sanctum from any vantage point.
