Waterworld & Liquid Gallery

An open-air gallery: square, half-sunk pool basins on a meadow, one liquid per pool, so every liquid material of the engine can be compared side by side under the same sun.

Layout

A 4 x 4 grid, centered on the origin. Rows go from realistic to exotic. Each pool has a signboard beside it.

Frames

Ceramic tile rims (procedural canvas textures). Palette follows the liquid: bright pool cyan for water, dark ceramics for dark fluids, basalt for lava, salt white for the Dead Sea, inked comic tiles for Noir.

Props

Each pool carries crates and balls on the floor, two bobbing floaters and a periodic dropper that splashes in. The two buoyancy pools use real rigid bodies riding the wave field, so the Dead Sea visibly floats them higher.

Tools

Golden-capture mode for deterministic screenshots (__golden=<pool>), and ?tune=1 for live tuning of the toon pool (keys 0, G, arrows).

Honest status

The gallery is a technical comparison, not a finished scene: lighting is one sun plus colored point lights at lava and slime, the meadow is a single tiled texture, and several older texture maps in the asset folder are unused.
